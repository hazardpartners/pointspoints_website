Why Points Points!

Your wallet should
make sense to you.

Rewards can be useful. Understanding them should be easier.

Points Points! is a product of Hazard Partners LLC.

You already have credit cards. You might have points, credits, and benefits too. But knowing what to use—and when—can mean piecing together different rules, values, and conditions.

Points Points! brings that picture into focus. It helps you compare estimated rewards, understand your benefits, and make choices around the cards you already own.

Useful by design.
Free by commitment.

Points Points! is always free to use. There’s no subscription and no in-app purchase required to get value from your wallet. Ever.

How we plan to keep it free.

We plan to earn money through referrals using affiliate networks and partnerships. If you choose a qualifying partner link, we may earn a commission. Affiliate compensation and partnerships never influence which cards we recommend or how we rank them.

We never sell your data because we don’t have your data. Our business model is referrals, not selling personal information.

Your personal information stays with you.

Your personal details, wallet selections, and rewards calculations stay with you. No account or bank connection required. We never sell user data.

We explain optional services and data handling in our privacy policy, so you can understand the details behind that approach.

Clarity beats a headline number.

A recommendation should explain its assumptions, acknowledge what’s unknown, and help you understand the tradeoffs. Your goals and preferences matter. The final choice stays with you.
