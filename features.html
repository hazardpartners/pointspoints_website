Make more of
what you already have.

Set goals for a more personal experience. Then compare your credit cards, discover benefits, and get optional Lock Screen suggestions when you arrive.

01 / Goals & personalization

Start with a goal.
Make rewards personal.

A trip on points. A hotel stay. A status milestone. Make supported recommendations reflect your goals.

  • Supported points and status goals
  • A personal goal name and a pin on Overview
  • Manual progress updates and program preferences

Goals are optional. Progress is recorded manually, without a loyalty-account connection.

Explore goals ↗

02 / Location-aware recommendations

Your best card.
Before you pay.

Get an optional Lock Screen card suggestion when you arrive at a supported place.

  • You choose whether to enable location and notifications.
  • Personalized card comparisons run on your device.
  • Nearby and Search help when the place isn’t clear.

Background arrival alerts require Always location access and notification permission, and depend on iOS delivery. You still select your payment card yourself.

Explore arrival alerts ↗

03 / Lock Screen widget

A little widget.
A quicker check.

See a suggested card at a glance with the optional Lock Screen widget.

About the Lock Screen widget ↗

Widget suggestions may show a previous result or an everyday default. iOS controls refresh timing.

04 / Card comparisons

A recommendation

The multiplier is the beginning.
The value is what matters.

05 / Ask + Apple Intelligence

Less digging.
Just Ask.

Not sure which card to use, how a perk works, or where to start with a goal? Open Ask and put it in your own words.

  • Explore your included benefits and your Wallet’s strengths
  • Get explanations grounded in your Wallet and supported rewards information
  • Use optional Apple Intelligence for supported explanations and goal drafts

Ask’s Apple Intelligence features require iOS 26 or later on a compatible iPhone, with Apple Intelligence enabled. Availability depends on device, language, region, and settings. Guided tools and rules-based answers remain available without Apple Intelligence.
