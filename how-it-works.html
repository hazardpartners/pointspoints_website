How it works

Your rewards.
More possibilities.

Compare your credit cards. Find useful perks. Work toward your goals.

01

Start with your wallet.

See your cards, rewards preferences, and benefits in one place.

  • ✓No Points Points! account required
  • ✓A wallet built around your cards
  • ✓Keep your wallet profile on your device

02

Set a goal.
Make it personal.

A trip on points. A hotel stay. A status milestone. Make supported recommendations reflect your goals.

Goals are optional. Progress is recorded manually, without a loyalty-account connection.

A goal worth working toward.

Points, travel, or status.

Comparisons that reflect you.

Your priorities and reward preferences.

Progress you can see.

A personal goal and manual updates.

03

Let the answer
come to you.

Get an optional card suggestion on your Lock Screen when you arrive at a supported place.

  • ✓Arrive at a supported place
  • ✓See the suggested card on your Lock Screen
  • ✓Understand why a card fits

The optional Lock Screen widget keeps a quick card suggestion close by.

Background alerts require Always location access and notifications, and depend on iOS delivery. Search works without arrival alerts.

04

Search whenever you need to.

Compare estimated rewards for a store, purchase, or everyday spending category.

  • ✓Search a store or purchase
  • ✓Browse everyday spending categories
  • ✓Compare your cards for that purchase

05

Explore what fits your life.

Discover cards, loyalty programs, travel perks, and entertainment tools.

  • ✓Browse cards and loyalty programs
  • ✓Find travel and entertainment tools
  • ✓Save useful perks for later

Your choice
stays yours.

Points Points! helps you compare. You choose the card to pay with and confirm any benefits or enrollment with your issuer.

The app does not move money, route payments, or automatically switch the active card in Apple Pay.
