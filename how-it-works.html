How it works

Less setup.
More understanding.

Add the cards you own. Enable optional arrival alerts. Get a helpful card recommendation on your Lock Screen, with Search ready whenever you need it.

01

Start with your wallet.

Open Wallet and choose Review Setup Guide to select the card products you own. Goals & Benefits and Advanced Point Valuations let you personalize your setup as you go.

  • ✓No Points Points account required
  • ✓Choose card products by name
  • ✓Keep your wallet profile on your device

02

Let the answer
come to you.

During app setup, enable automatic recommendations and allow notifications. Enable Always location access for arrival recommendations while the app is in the background.

  • ✓Arrive at a supported place
  • ✓See the suggested card on your Lock Screen
  • ✓Tap for the explanation; choose your payment card yourself

Add the optional Best Card Here widget to your Lock Screen for a quick way to open a nearby check.

You can change the automatic recommendation setting in Current Location and manage location and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

If the place isn’t clear or an alert isn’t delivered, open Nearby to choose a place or use Search. These features also work when you leave arrival alerts off.

03

Search whenever you need to.

Open Search and enter a store or purchase, or choose a category such as Dining, Grocery, Gas, or Travel. Start here when you want to compare which card to use.

  • ✓Search a store or purchase
  • ✓Browse everyday spending categories
  • ✓Compare your cards for that purchase

04

Explore what fits your life.

Open Explore to browse credit cards, loyalty programs, tips, and merchants. Travel and Entertainment lead to tools for looking at trips and subscription choices in more detail.

  • ✓Browse cards and loyalty programs
  • ✓Find travel and entertainment tools
  • ✓Save useful perks for later

Your choice
stays yours.

Points Points helps you compare. You choose the card to pay with and confirm any benefits or enrollment with your issuer.

The app does not move money, route payments, or automatically switch the active card in Apple Pay.
