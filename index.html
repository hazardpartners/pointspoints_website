The right card. Right where you are.

Walk in.
Know which card.

Arrive at a supported store or restaurant, and Points Points! can recommend a card from your Wallet on your Lock Screen. The answer is in the notification; tap for the explanation.

Enable automatic recommendations, notifications, and background location in the app. Your cards are compared on your iPhone using the reward rules and preferences available there.

See how arrival alerts work ↗

Optional and permission-based. Alerts depend on place confidence and iOS background delivery. Search and Nearby are always there when you need them.

Your Lock Screen, a little smarter

A little widget.
A quicker check.

Add the Points Points! Best Card Here widget to your iPhone Lock Screen. See a compact card label at a glance, then tap to open Points Points! and check nearby.

Keep the widget beneath your clock and arrival recommendations in your notifications. A helpful card suggestion is close at hand when you’re ready to pay.

About the Lock Screen widget ↗

iOS controls widget refresh timing. The widget can show a last-known recommendation or an everyday default until a new nearby check is available.

Kept with you.
