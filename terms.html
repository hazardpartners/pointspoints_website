Using Points Points!

These terms describe use of the Points Points! website and app. Points Points! helps explain credit card rewards and benefits. It does not operate your financial accounts, move money, route payments, or automatically select your active Apple Pay card.

Use the service lawfully and avoid entering payment credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information. The app may also be subject to the applicable App Store license terms presented with its distribution.

Always free to use

Points Points! is always free to use. No subscription or in-app purchase is required. Your device, internet service, cards, merchants, and other third-party products may have their own charges, including card annual fees. Those charges are not fees for using Points Points!

Reward estimates & your decisions

Reward values, comparisons, and recommendations are informational estimates, not financial, credit, tax, or legal advice. They depend on available rules, personal valuations, merchant classification, eligibility, and the details you supply. No estimate guarantees approval, a credit, a reward, a redemption value, or savings.

Verify important facts with your issuer or program before acting. Enrollment, caps, provider restrictions, billing paths, fees, and changing terms can affect the outcome. Do not base a purchase or card application solely on a displayed estimate.
